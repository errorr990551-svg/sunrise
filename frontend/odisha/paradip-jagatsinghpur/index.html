Why Paradip buys nipples for hydrocarbons, bulk dust and salt spray

Paradip's refinery is a large, modern complex, running crude units, conversion units, a hydrogen plant and utilities, and it is expanding into petrochemicals. It goes through planned turnarounds like any refinery, and each one brings contractors, a materials list and tight deadlines. The environment adds three more demands. Salt: spray and humidity attack exposed pipework. Weather: monsoon rain is heavy and cyclone seasons are real. And dust: coal, ore and fertiliser dust settles into threads.

Buying Guide

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for refinery, port and fertiliser buyers

01

What exactly is in the line?

Name the fluid, concentration and temperature. Hydrocarbon, steam, seawater-affected water and fertiliser-plant chemicals each call for different materials. Some acid and ammonia services need alloys beyond stainless. We'll say plainly if a duty goes beyond what we supply.

02

Is it outdoors?

Paradip's salt air and cyclone rain attack standard 304 within a season. Choose 316 or 316L for anything outdoors or exposed.

03

When is the turnaround or project milestone?

Parts must be on site before the window opens. Send the full list and date, and we'll say honestly what we can meet. Contractors should tell us what documents their client requires.

04

What thread?

Refinery and fertiliser instruments often use NPT, while local pipework uses BSPT. They don't interchange. Send a photo or part number.

Local Realities

What Salt, Cyclones and Bulk Dust Do to Metal

Five realities of the Paradip coast

Chloride pitting

Salt air and spray attack the protective film on stainless. 304 can stain and pit. 316 and 316L resist this far better.

Cyclone seasons

Storms bring wind, salt and flooding, and this coast has taken direct hits. Secure lines, and check threads after a storm.

Bulk-cargo dust

Coal, ore and fertiliser dust settles into threads and fouls seals. Wipe clean before assembly, and keep spares sealed.

Turnaround and project deadlines

Missing small parts delay restart or milestones. Send lists weeks ahead.

A very long haul from Ahmedabad

Paradip is among our farthest markets. Holding a few extra fast-moving sizes is cheap insurance.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Stainless Nipples in Salt and Dust

Habits that extend life on the Paradip coast

  • Seal every thread fully with a suitable tape or paste, so salt water can't creep in.
  • Use a sealant compatible with the fluid. Check against solvents, acids and temperature.
  • Use anti-seize on stainless threads you'll open for inspection.
  • Don't over-tighten. Stainless can gall, and damaged threads trap salt.
  • Keep carbon-steel tools away from stainless surfaces.
  • Rinse and dry exposed joints after installation if salt spray is likely.
  • Inspect on a schedule for rust streaks, weeping and salt crust.
