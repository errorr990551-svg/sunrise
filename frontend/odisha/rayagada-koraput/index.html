Regional Analysis

Bauxite, Alumina, Paper and Aero Engines

Why this belt buys nipples by chemistry, by drawing and by the monsoon

Alumina refining is an unusually hard-on-metal process. Crushed bauxite is digested in hot, strong caustic soda under pressure, red mud is separated and washed, and alumina hydrate is precipitated and calcined. The pipework carries hot caustic liquor, steam and slurry. Carbon steel is the usual material for much of it, because stainless steels can crack under tension in hot strong caustic, so material choice here is a matter for the process designer, not the buyer. Instrument lines, utilities, cooling water, DM water and compressed air offer more room for stainless. The Panchpatmali mines and conveyor systems add hydraulics, lubrication and dust-suppression water.

At Jaykaypur, the paper mill's pulping, bleaching and steam systems add another chemistry, with effluent-treatment plants on the same site. At Sunabeda, the aerospace division and its vendors machine parts to revision-controlled drawings, with demanding tolerances and records. Rayagada, Koraput and Gunupur add hospitals, hotels, tribal-area government works and a large trade in coffee, turmeric and forest produce.

The monsoon here is among the heaviest in the state, and landslides can close roads.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Nipples in Alkaline, Dusty and Wet Conditions

Habits for plant and mine crews

  • Use a sealant compatible with the fluid. Check against caustic, solvents and temperature.
  • Use a high-temperature sealant on steam lines, and anti-seize on stainless threads.
  • Don't over-tighten. Tapered threads seal by wedging, and stainless can gall.
  • Clean threads before assembly, with no cutting oil, dirt or swarf.
  • Use only the materials your process specification allows on caustic and chemical lines.
  • Support long runs so vibration doesn't loosen joints.
  • Record the grade and thread of replaced parts for the next audit.
