Buying Guide

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for smelter, paper, port and rice-mill buyers

01

Is it outdoors or in salty air?

The north coast's salt, rain and wind attack standard 304 within a season. Choose 316 or 316L for outdoor and exposed lines.

02

Could it flood?

Cyclone surges and river floods put low-lying plants under water. Stainless and well-sealed threads recover far better than bare mild steel. Flush and dry lines afterwards.

03

Is it a restart after a storm?

Plants that stop for a cyclone need spares fast. Keep a kit of common sizes on site, and tell us what you'd want in it.

04

What documents does your customer need?

Defence-range vendors and port contractors face audits. Tell us before you order, and we'll confirm what accompanies the material.

Local Realities

What Cyclones, Floods and Salt Do to Pipework

Five realities of Balasore and Bhadrak

Cyclone landfall

Storms bring wind, salt and flooding, and plants shut and restart. Secure outdoor lines, and check threads after each storm.

Floods

River and surge floods reach low-lying sheds. Stainless recovers better. Flush and dry afterwards.

Salt air

Estuary and sea air attack the protective film on stainless. 304 can stain and pit. 316 and 316L resist this far better.

Humid monsoon

Moisture and condensate rust mild steel on lines that stay damp.

A very long haul from Ahmedabad

Balasore is among our farthest markets. Holding a storm-season kit of fast-moving sizes is cheap insurance.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Stainless Nipples Where Storms Strike

Habits that extend life in salt, wind and flood

  • Seal every thread fully with a suitable tape or paste, so salt water can't creep in.
  • Use a high-temperature sealant on steam lines, and anti-seize on stainless threads.
  • Don't over-tighten. Stainless can gall, and damaged threads trap salt.
  • Raise vulnerable lines off the floor where you can.
  • Flush and dry lines after a flood, and replace any rust-affected mild-steel nipples.
  • Keep carbon-steel tools away from stainless surfaces.
  • Inspect after each storm for weeping and salt crust.
