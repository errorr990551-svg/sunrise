Why this district buys nipples for takt time, dye houses and every litre of water

The Penukonda plant has changed the district's industrial map. The thread tying them together is water. Anantapur's rainfall is among the lowest in India, borewells are deep, and recycling is the rule, so every weeping fitting counts.

Buying Guide

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for car-plant suppliers, mills and dye houses

01

Which revision is the drawing?

Auto buyers work with revision-controlled drawings. Send the revision with your request, so we machine to the right one.

02

What tolerance and finish?

Tell us thread class, length tolerance and surface finish. If your inspector gauges the thread, tell us. We plan inspection around what you check.

03

Is there salt or dye in the fluid?

Silk dyeing uses salt and chemicals, which attack 304 over time. Use 316 on liquor and effluent lines. For clean steam and water, 304 is enough.

04

Where is your water coming from?

Plants here recycle and measure water carefully. A weeping nipple wastes what you've worked to secure. Choose stainless for lines that stay wet.

Local Realities

What a Dry District Demands of Its Pipework

Five realities of a thirsty, fast-industrialising district

Water scarcity

One of India's driest districts treats every litre as an asset. Recycling systems have many joints, and a leak costs more than the fitting.

Batch-to-batch consistency

Tier-1 suppliers answer to a carmaker's audits. Batch twelve has to match batch one.

Heat above 43 degrees

Summer heat bakes sealants and loosens joints. Use sealants rated for the temperature.

Dye-house chemistry

Silk dyeing and finishing use salt and chemicals. Match the grade to the liquor.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Nipples on Air, Coolant, Dye and Recycled-Water Lines

Habits that cut leaks and rework

  • Fit drain points at low spots in compressed-air lines.
  • Use sealant suited to the fluid. PTFE tape works for air and water, but check compatibility for oils, coolants and chemicals.
  • Use anti-seize on stainless threads that will be opened again.
  • Don't over-tighten. Tapered threads seal by wedging, and stainless can gall.
  • Leak-hunt recycled-water lines regularly.
  • Support long drops. Vibration from compressors and machines loosens joints.
  • Label thread types on mixed BSPT and NPT lines.
