Coal, Containers, Prawns and Rice

Why this coast buys nipples for seawater, ash and aeration

Krishnapatnam is a private deep-water port that handles coal, containers, iron ore, fertiliser and other bulk cargo, and an industrial city is planned around it. Large thermal power plants stand nearby, one of them state-owned at Muthukur and others privately run on the coast, and their cooling systems draw on seawater. Contractors for boilers, coal handling, ash handling and port equipment live in the area, and the port's expansion keeps construction going.

Nellore district is one of India's top producers of farmed shrimp and fish, with hatcheries, feed mills, processing plants and ice plants strung along the coast and the Pennar delta. The city is also a rice trading and milling centre, with processing units that run steam and hot water. Naidupeta and Gudur add pipe, steel, electronics, food and agro industries, and Venkatachalam and Sullurpeta carry port and highway logistics.

The climate defines the work. Salt air attacks outdoor fittings quickly, heavy monsoon and cyclone rains soak everything and summer heat bakes sealants.

Buying Guide

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for port, power, aqua and mill buyers

01

Is it outdoors or touching seawater?

Port and seawater lines face salt, brine and wind-borne spray. Choose 316 or 316L. For permanently immersed or hot seawater service, ask your engineer whether a higher alloy is needed.

02

Is it in ash or coal dust?

Ash slurry is abrasive and wears fittings from inside. Choose a sturdy wall and inspect on a schedule.

03

Is it a refrigeration line?

Ice plants and cold stores often use ammonia. Never use brass or copper alloys on ammonia. Follow your refrigeration designer's specification.

04

When is the shutdown or project date?

Parts must be on site before the window opens. Send the full list and date, and we'll say honestly what we can meet.

Local Realities

What Salt, Cyclones and Ash Do to Metal

Five realities of the Nellore coast

Chloride pitting

Salt air and seawater attack the protective film on stainless. 304 can stain and pit. 316 and 316L resist this far better.

Cyclone season

Storms between October and December bring wind, salt and flooding, and this coast has been hit before. Secure lines, and check threads after a storm.

Abrasive ash and coal dust

Power-plant dust and slurry wear fittings. Wipe clean before assembly, and inspect often.

Aqua-farm wash-down and aeration

Brine and constant water use stress every joint. Choose smooth finishes and corrosion-resistant grades.

Very long supply lines

Nellore is far from Ahmedabad. Send complete lists and dates so we can consolidate dispatch.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Stainless Nipples on the Nellore Coast

Habits that extend life in salt, ash and wet

  • Seal every thread fully with a suitable tape or paste, so salt water can't creep in.
  • Use a high-temperature sealant on steam lines, and anti-seize on stainless threads.
  • Don't over-tighten. Stainless can gall, and damaged threads trap salt.
  • Keep carbon-steel tools away from stainless surfaces.
  • Use only the materials your designer allows on ammonia lines.
  • Rinse and dry exposed joints after installation if salt spray is likely.
  • Inspect on a schedule for rust streaks, weeping and salt crust.
