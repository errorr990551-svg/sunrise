Regional Analysis

Steam Kitchens, Imported Production Lines and Mango Season

Why this district buys nipples for volume cooking, commissioning dates and fruit pulp

Tirupati's kitchens are among the largest anywhere. The temple's prasadam kitchens and pilgrim canteens cook for tens of thousands of people a day, using steam boilers, large cooking vessels, dish-washing lines and hot-water systems that run for long hours. Around them sit hundreds of hotels, lodges and guest houses, the university and its medical institutes, and big hospitals drawing patients from several states. All of it uses a great deal of water, and pilgrim peaks, festivals and special days push every system to its limit.

Sri City and the Tada–Srikalahasti belt look nothing like that. These are new, orderly industrial estates, where plants arrive with imported machines, strict drawings and fixed commissioning dates. Automotive, electronics, appliance, FMCG and food factories fit out their utility lines in one go, and tier-2 suppliers follow them.

Chittoor, Puttur and Nagari complete the picture. Mango-pulp units run hot-fill and steam lines in the fruit season, and a large dairy and agro belt feeds from the surrounding villages.

Buying Guide

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for kitchen, factory and pulp-line buyers

01

How heavy is the daily use?

Pilgrim kitchens run steam and hot water for many hours, every day. Choose a heavier wall for steam, stainless for hidden joints and clean threads for fittings that open often.

02

Do you have the full size list?

New factories and hospital wings buy dozens of sizes at once. Send the list from your piping drawing, and we'll quote it as one order and one dispatch.

03

What does the imported equipment need?

Production lines imported for new plants often use NPT or other threads, while site pipework is BSPT. They don't interchange. Send a photo or part number.

04

When is the peak or the commissioning date?

Pilgrim peaks, the mango season and plant start-ups run to dates. Tell us yours, and we'll plan backward from it.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Nipples in Kitchens, Plant Rooms and New Factories

Habits that save callbacks

  • Use a high-temperature sealant on steam lines, and a food-safe compound on food-adjacent lines.
  • Use anti-seize on stainless threads you open for cleaning.
  • Don't over-tighten. Heat adds stress, and stainless can gall.
  • Wipe off machining oil before commissioning clean utilities, and flush.
  • Pressure-test every loop before it goes live and before closing walls.
  • Check every thread type against the equipment before bulk assembly.
  • Label thread types on mixed BSPT and NPT lines.
