Foundries, Galvanising and a Thousand Lathes

Why Howrah buys stainless, specials and stock, and not just plain nipples

Howrah is West Bengal's old engineering heartland. Foundries and casting machine shops cluster around Bally, Liluah and Belur. Galvanising units, wire-drawing mills, bolt and nut makers, rolling mills and small steel fabricators fill Dasnagar, Andul Road and Sankrail. Behind them runs a dense web of one-room workshops that turn, drill and finish for larger firms. A food park at Sankrail and jute and paper units along the river add other users. Howrah's local fitting manufacturers supply much of eastern India with mild-steel and galvanised nipples.

That shapes how we compete. We don't pretend to beat a Howrah workshop on a plain galvanised nipple. What we offer is stainless nipples in the grades and sizes local shops don't carry, brass nipples, controlled-dimension custom parts, and stainless pipe and bar stock for makers who would rather machine their own.

In a market this experienced, a supplier earns trade by being useful at the edges.

Buying Guide

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for makers who know their trade

01

Finished part or raw stock?

If you machine your own, buy stainless pipe or hex bar from us and cut it to your own sizes. If you need a few special pieces, buy them finished. Tell us which.

02

Why stainless?

Galvanising, pickling and processing lines are harsh, and mild steel and zinc coatings don't last. Stainless costs more at first and less over a season.

03

Which thread?

Local equipment mostly uses BSPT. Imported machines bring NPT. They don't interchange. Send a photo or part number.

04

How many, and how often?

Dealers and workshops should send top sizes and monthly volumes so we can quote assortments or scheduled supply.

Local Realities

What Howrah’s Workshops and Foundries Demand

Five realities of an old engineering town

Sharp price awareness

Howrah buyers know what a nipple costs to make. We don’t promise the lowest number. We aim to be useful where local supply stops.

Heat, sand and dust

Foundry sand and dust work into threads. Clean threads before fitting, and use sealants rated for the temperature.

Acid fumes

Galvanising and pickling lines attack steel and zinc quickly. Choose stainless for utilities nearby.

Humid, flood-prone lanes

Monsoon waterlogging reaches low-lying workshops. Stainless and sealed threads recover better.

Long supply from Gujarat

Howrah is a long haul. We suit buyers who can combine orders, take raw stock or order specials that local shops don’t make.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Nipples in Howrah Plants

Habits that save downtime, prevent callbacks and ensure leak-free runs

  • Clean threads before every assembly. Sand, grit and old sealant cause leaks.
  • Use a high-temperature sealant on steam lines, and anti-seize on stainless threads.
  • Don’t over-tighten. Cast housings crack and stainless galls.
  • Keep stainless apart from carbon-steel tools to avoid embedded rust particles.
  • Support long runs so vibration doesn’t loosen joints.
  • Keep a small spares kit of the sizes that fail most.
  • Label thread types on mixed BSPT and NPT lines.
