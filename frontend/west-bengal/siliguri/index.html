Regional Analysis

Tea, Timber and a Trade Crossroads

Why Siliguri buys nipples for dryers, boilers and a regional market

Siliguri is a commercial hub more than a heavy-industrial one. Its auction centre handles a large share of the tea grown in the Terai and Dooars, packers and blenders operate across the Fulbari, Matigara and Dabgram areas, and a cluster of tea-machinery makers builds and repairs withering, rolling and drying equipment for gardens across the region. The factories themselves sit mostly in the tea belt of Jalpaiguri, Dhupguri and Alipurduar, where boilers, heat exchangers and hot-air dryers run through the plucking season. Plywood and veneer units, food and beverage processors and cold-chain operators add further steam and hot-water demand.

Siliguri's other business is serving people. The North Bengal Medical College and private hospitals draw patients from several states and countries, hotels and travel firms support the hill tourism, and the wholesale hardware and plumbing trade supplies contractors and shops across the hills, Sikkim and the Northeast.

The climate shapes the supply. Monsoon rain here is among the heaviest in Bengal, humidity is high, and winters in the tea gardens are cool and quiet, which is when most factories shut for annual maintenance.

Buying Guide

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for tea, hospital and trade buyers

01

Is it a tea-factory line?

Boiler and dryer lines need a heavier wall and a high-temperature sealant. Tea dust is fine and dry, so keep threads clean. Tell us the pressure and fluid, and ask your engineer to confirm what a threaded joint may carry.

02

When is the winter overhaul?

Most tea factories shut for the cool season and maintain. Parts must be on site before that window opens. Send the list in good time.

03

Is the line wet most of the year?

Siliguri’s rain and humidity rust mild steel on damp lines. Stainless and sealed threads last longer.

Local Realities

What North Bengal’s Rain and Seasons Demand

Five realities of tea country and the foothills

Heavy monsoon and humidity

Rain falls for months and keeps lines damp. Seal threads fully, and choose stainless where lines stay wet.

Plucking season and winter shutdown

Tea factories run hard through the season and shut in the cool months. A steam failure in season is costly, so overhaul before it starts.

Boilers on long shifts

Daily heat cycles loosen threaded joints over time. Check them on a schedule.

Hill-road logistics

Landslides and rain can slow deliveries into the hills. Dealers should stock ahead of the monsoon.

A very long haul from Ahmedabad

Siliguri is among our farthest markets. Send complete lists and dates so we can consolidate dispatch.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Nipples in Siliguri Plants

Habits that save downtime, prevent callbacks and ensure leak-free runs

  • Use a high-temperature sealant on steam lines, and anti-seize on stainless threads.
  • Don’t over-tighten. Heat adds stress to tapered threads.
  • Seal threads fully on lines exposed to rain and humidity.
  • Clean threads before assembly, especially in dusty dryer areas.
  • Allow for expansion with supports or flexible sections.
  • Re-check tightness after the first heat cycles and at season start.
  • Label thread types on mixed BSPT and NPT lines.
