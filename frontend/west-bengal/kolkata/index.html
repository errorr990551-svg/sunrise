Regional Analysis

Heritage Plumbing, Leather Tanneries and a Wall of Data Centres

Why Kolkata buys nipples for repairs, hospitals and hardware shelves

Kolkata is among India's oldest industrial cities, and its demand shows it. In the old city, around Burrabazar, Chitpur and Canning Street, the hardware and metal trade has run for generations, supplying contractors, mechanics and workshops across eastern India. Heritage buildings, old hospitals, hotels and clubs need regular plumbing repair, often in awkward sizes. East of the city, Salt Lake's Sector V and New Town carry IT campuses, data centres and large private hospitals. The Bantala leather complex houses tanneries with a common effluent treatment plant, and the Taratala, Dum Dum and Kolkata Port areas add engineering, marine and logistics. Hotels, malls and apartment towers keep growing.

Three conditions mark Kolkata. First, humidity: the air is wet for much of the year, and a heavy monsoon follows. Second, water: parts of the city and its neighbouring districts deal with waterlogged streets in the rains and with groundwater problems that have led to extensive treatment plants. Third, age: old buildings mean unusual threads, odd lengths and hidden pipes.

Sunrise Industries supplies for all three.

Buying Guide

Five Questions Before You Order

Written for contractors, tanneries and dealers

01

Is it an old-building repair?

Send the old nipple, or a measured sketch with thread pitch and length, and we'll machine a match. Old pipework often uses odd lengths and worn threads.

02

Will the joint be hidden?

Pipes behind walls and in risers are costly to reach. Use stainless for hidden joints, so you don't open a finished surface for a corroded fitting.

03

How wet is the location?

Kolkata's humidity and monsoon keep basement and ground-level pipework wet. Choose stainless and seal threads fully.

04

What is in the line?

Tannery and effluent lines carry chromium salts, acids and chlorides, where compatibility decides the grade. Name the chemical, concentration and temperature, and we'll say honestly if a duty needs more than we supply.

05

How many sizes at once?

Contractors should send the whole list. Dealers should send top sizes and monthly volumes. One quote, one dispatch.

Kolkata's high humidity and monsoon waterlogging corrode mild steel quickly. Stainless SS 304 is the default standard for long-term piping, while SS 316/316L protects chemical and effluent installations.

Local Realities

What Kolkata's Climate and Age Do to Pipework

Five realities of a humid, old and growing city

Humidity year-round

Wet air and a long monsoon rust mild steel quickly. Stainless and sealed threads last longer.

Waterlogging & flooding

Streets and basements can flood in heavy rain. Lines near the floor need corrosion-resistant grades.

Odd old threads

Heritage pipework often uses non-standard sizes. Custom machining from a sample solves it.

Always-on cooling loops

Data centres and hospitals run chilled water around the clock. One failed joint above live equipment is a serious problem.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Nipples in Kolkata Plants

Habits that save downtime, prevent callbacks and ensure leak-free runs

  • Use the right sealant. PTFE tape suits many cold-water joints. Hot-water and steam joints need a sealant rated for the temperature.
  • Hold the nipple by its hex while tightening the fitting.
  • Don’t over-tighten. Old cast fittings crack and stainless can gall.
  • Use anti-seize on stainless threads you will reopen.
  • Pressure-test before closing walls and ceilings.
  • Seal threads fully in basements and wet areas.
  • Label thread types on mixed BSPT and NPT lines.
