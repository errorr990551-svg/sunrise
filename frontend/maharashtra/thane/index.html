Buying Guide

Questions to Ask Before You Order in Thane

Tailored specifically for Thane's industrial conditions

01

What exactly flows through the line?

Name the chemical, concentration and temperature. Hot chlorides, strong acids and strong oxidisers can attack even 316L. Tell us the details.

02

Is it welded in after fitting?

If a nipple will be welded into an assembly, 316L and 304L avoid carbide precipitation and sensitisation that affect standard grades.

03

What documentation does your audit need?

Pharma and chemical audits ask for material test reports. Tell us before you order so MTC 3.1 is included.

04

What thread and schedule wall?

Imported reactors and instruments often use NPT, while local piping uses BSPT. Heavy-wall Schedule 80 suits steam and jacket duty.

05

Is it a process line or a building line?

Contractors should send full size lists; process buyers should specify grade and pressure.

Metallurgy Guide

SS 304, SS 316, MS or Brass: Thane Edition

Match the material to the line environment, not just the upfront budget

Material Works Well When… Think Twice When…
SS 316L Chemical, solvent, scrubber, pharma, dye and welded lines Very strong hot acids (check corrosion chart)
SS 316 Process, instrument and brine lines Where welded without post-weld annealing
SS 304 Utility water, steam, chilled water, building services Chloride-rich or aggressive chemical lines
Brass Dry compressed air Ammonia, acids, solvents

For batch reactors undergoing repeated solvent flushes and steam cycles, low-carbon SS 316L is standard practice to prevent intergranular stress corrosion cracking.

Operational Realities

What the Thane–Kalyan Chemical Belt Demands

Five realities of batch chemistry and a crowded city

⚙️

Compatibility first

A grade that works for one solvent or acid can fail in another. Match the material to the fluid, not just habit.

⚙️

Frequent batch changeovers

Reactors are opened, cleaned and recharged often. Threads that open repeatedly need anti-seize and proper care.

⚙️

Heat cycles on reactor jackets

Steam and thermic-fluid jackets heat and cool every batch, which loosens joints over time. Re-check them regularly.

⚙️

Strict audit & safety scrutiny

Plants are audited stringently. Weeping joints on chemical lines draw safety citations. Choose the right grade and keep records.

⚙️

Small, frequent urgent orders

A batch plant may need five nipples urgently to restart. Tell us what is critical, and we will quote stock dispatch.

Field Fitting Advice

Fitting Stainless Nipples in Thane Without Headaches

Practical field habits that save plant hours and prevent leaks

🔧

Tip 1

Use a sealant compatible with the fluid: PTFE tape suits many lines, but verify against solvents, acids and operating temperature.

🔧

Tip 2

Use anti-seize on stainless threads you will reopen.

🔧

Tip 3

Don't over-tighten: Tapered threads seal by wedging, and stainless can gall.

🔧

Tip 4

Clean and degrease threads and bores before commissioning.

🔧

Tip 5

Leak-test with an appropriate inert fluid before introducing process chemicals.

🔧

Tip 6

Record the grade and thread of replaced parts for audit logs.

🔧

Tip 7

Mark thread type on mixed BSPT and NPT lines.
