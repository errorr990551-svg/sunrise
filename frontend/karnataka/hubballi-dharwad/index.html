Metallurgy Guide

SS 304, SS 316, MS or Brass: Hubballi–Dharwad Edition

Match the material to the line environment, not just the upfront budget

Material Works Well When… Think Twice When…
Mild steel Dry hydraulic and compressed-air lines Wet, hygienic or hot-water lines
SS 304 Cooling water, steam, agro-processing, plumbing Chloride-rich or chemical lines
SS 316 Dosing and harsher chemistry Cost-sensitive plain lines
Brass Pneumatics, low-pressure water Steam with alkali, ammonia

Heavy-wall MS serves dry hydraulic and workshop air lines reliably. For foundry cooling water and agro steam, SS 304 prevents rust and scaling.

Field Fitting Advice

Fitting Stainless Nipples in Hubballi–Dharwad Without Headaches

Practical field habits that save plant hours and prevent leaks

🔧

Tip 1

Clean threads before every assembly. Dust and old sealant cause leaks.

🔧

Tip 2

Use a high-temperature sealant on steam lines, and anti-seize on stainless threads.

🔧

Tip 3

Don’t over-tighten. Tapered threads seal by wedging, and cast housings crack.

🔧

Tip 4

Support long runs so vibration doesn’t loosen joints.

🔧

Tip 5

Pressure-test air and hydraulic lines before returning to service, following your procedure.

🔧

Tip 6

Label thread types on mixed BSPT and NPT lines.

🔧

Tip 7

Keep spares dry and sealed.
