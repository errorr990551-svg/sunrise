Buying Guide

Questions to Ask Before You Order in Bengaluru

Tailored specifically for Bengaluru's industrial conditions

01

How many, and how identical?

Facility contractors use the same nipple hundreds of times, and consistency of thread and dimension matters most. Job shops want a few of many sizes. Send the full list, and we'll quote it as one order.

02

Do you need stainless at all?

Bengaluru's mild, inland climate means 304 is plenty for most water, coolant and air lines, and 316 is rarely needed unless the fluid is corrosive. For dry painted lines, MS can still be right. We'll tell you where stainless earns its price.

03

What does the specification say?

Aerospace and pharma buyers work to drawings, revisions and sometimes documents. Send the revision, tolerance and finish, and tell us what paperwork you need.

04

Which thread?

Local pipework mostly uses BSPT, while imported equipment brings NPT. They don't interchange. Send a photo or part number.

05

What's the delivery window?

Bengaluru is a long haul from Ahmedabad. Give us the date up front, and we'll plan backward from it.

Metallurgy Guide

SS 304, SS 316, MS or Brass: Bengaluru Edition

Match the material to the line environment, not just the upfront budget

Material Works Well When… Think Twice When…
SS 304 Coolant, chilled water, air, general utility Chloride-rich or strongly chemical lines
SS 316 / 316L Pharma, dosing, harsher cleaning chemistry Cost-sensitive plain lines
Brass Pneumatics, low-pressure air and water Ammonia, strong chemicals
Mild steel Dry painted air and coolant lines Wet, hidden or hygienic lines

Bengaluru's inland climate means SS 304 handles almost all cooling and utility duty. Choose SS 316 for clean pharma dosing and aggressive chemicals.

Field Fitting Advice

Fitting Stainless Nipples in Bengaluru Without Headaches

Practical field habits that save plant hours and prevent leaks

🔧

Tip 1

Use sealant suited to the fluid. PTFE tape works for air and water, but check compatibility for coolants and oils.

🔧

Tip 2

Use anti-seize on stainless threads that will be opened again.

🔧

Tip 3

Don't over-tighten. Tapered threads seal by wedging, and stainless can gall.

🔧

Tip 4

Pressure-test every loop before it goes live, especially above equipment.

🔧

Tip 5

Support long runs so vibration doesn't loosen joints.

🔧

Tip 6

Label thread types on mixed BSPT and NPT lines.

🔧

Tip 7

Keep a few spares of the sizes you reorder most.
