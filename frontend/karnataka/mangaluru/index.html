Regional Analysis

A Port City With a Refinery, Fertiliser Plants and Cashew Factories

Why Mangaluru's pipework faces salt from outside and chemistry from inside

Mangaluru is Karnataka’s main port and its chemical and petrochemical gateway. The New Mangalore Port at Panambur handles bulk cargo, petroleum, fertiliser and containers. Around it sit a major refinery and petrochemical complex, fertiliser and chemical plants, and storage terminals. The Baikampady industrial area hosts seafood processing, cold storage, ice plants, engineering units and cashew processing, while Bajpe and Mulky add more manufacturing. The city is also a major healthcare and education centre, with large hospitals and hotels. Puttur and the Dakshina Kannada hinterland add cashew, arecanut and rubber processing.

Coastal Karnataka also receives some of the heaviest monsoon rainfall in India, and the air carries salt year-round. For metal, that’s an unforgiving combination.

Nipples here need to survive salt spray, humidity, wash-down and often chemicals. That points to 316 or 316L for most outdoor and process lines, with 304 for sheltered utilities.

Is it outside, or in salty air?

Anything outdoors in Mangaluru faces salt, humidity and monsoon. Choose 316 or 316L. Standard 304 can tea-stain and pit within a season.

Refinery, fertiliser and chemical lines carry fluids where compatibility decides the grade. Name the chemical, concentration and temperature, and we’ll tell you honestly if a duty needs more than we supply.

Operational Realities

What Salt, Monsoon and Humidity Do to Metal

Five realities of the Mangaluru coast

🌊

Chloride pitting

Salt air and brine attack the protective film on stainless. 304 can pit and streak. 316 and 316L resist this far better.

🌧️

Monsoon for months

Rain falls heavily from June to September, and outdoor pipework stays wet. Seal threads fully, and inspect after the monsoon.

⚡

Crevice corrosion in threads

Threads trap salt water. Seal them properly, keep them clean and avoid leaving gaps.

⚙️

Wash-down culture

Seafood and cashew plants wash down constantly. Smooth finishes and corrosion-resistant grades keep lines clean.

⏱️

Mixed metals in humid air

Stainless in contact with galvanised or carbon steel in damp, salty conditions can corrode at the joint. Isolate dissimilar metals where possible.

Field Fitting Advice

Fitting Stainless Nipples in Mangaluru Without Headaches

Practical field habits that save plant hours and prevent leaks

🔧

Tip 1

Seal every thread with a suitable tape or paste, fully, so salt water can’t creep in.

🔧

Tip 2

Use anti-seize on stainless threads you’ll open for inspection.

🔧

Tip 3

Don’t over-tighten. Stainless can gall, and damaged threads trap salt.

🔧

Tip 4

Keep carbon-steel tools away from stainless surfaces.

🔧

Tip 5

Rinse and dry exposed joints after installation if salt spray is likely.

🔧

Tip 6

Inspect on a schedule for rust streaks, weeping and white salt crust.

🔧

Tip 7

Support long outdoor runs against wind and vibration.
