Ordering Questions

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for pump and machinery makers

1. Brass or stainless?

Domestic water pumps often use brass because it machines easily and costs less. Stainless suits industrial, corrosive, drinking-water-quality or food-adjacent duty. If the pump handles water with chlorine, chemicals or abrasive content, stainless is the safer choice.

2. Which thread does this model need?

Coimbatore pumps ship all over India and abroad. Domestic models mostly use BSP or BSPT, export models often use NPT or other standards. Send the customer's specification, and we'll quote accordingly. NPT has a 60° thread angle and BSP has 55°, so they don't swap.

3. How much pressure?

Schedule 40 works for most pump outlets. For high-head or industrial pumps, consider Schedule 80.

4. Is it a one-off or a repeat part?

Tell us annual quantity and delivery rhythm. For repeat parts, we can plan scheduled dispatches and keep stock ready in Ahmedabad.

Assembly Lines

Fitting Nipples on Pumps Without Rework

Habits for assembly lines and service teams

  • • Check thread engagement. A nipple that starts easily but bottoms out early may be the wrong thread family.
  • • Seal tapered threads properly. PTFE tape or compatible paste, applied in the thread direction, prevents weeping at the pump port.
  • • Torque gently on cast housings. Over-tightening a nipple into a cast pump body can crack it.
  • • Mind vibration. Pumps vibrate. A long nipple carrying a heavy gauge or valve may need support.
  • • Avoid dissimilar-metal damp joints where possible. Brass nipples in stainless housings, or the reverse, in constantly wet service can corrode over time.
  • • Flush before commissioning to clear machining swarf from the line.
