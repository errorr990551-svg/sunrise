Buying Guide

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for API, chemical and effluent buyers

01

What exactly is in the line?

Name the chemical, concentration and temperature. 316L suits many solvents, acids and alkalis, but not all. Some strong acids and hot chloride solutions attack even 316L. Send the details, and we'll tell you honestly if a duty needs a higher alloy than we supply.

02

Is it batch or continuous?

Batch lines open and close often and need anti-seize and care. Continuous lines favour heavier walls and fewer joints.

03

What documentation do audits need?

Pharma customers and regulators ask for material records. Tell us before you order, and we'll confirm what accompanies the material.

04

What thread and wall?

Imported reactors and instruments often use NPT, while local pipework uses BSPT. Heavy-wall Schedule 80 suits steam and jacket duty.

Operational Realities

What an Inland Chemical Belt Demands

Five realities of batch chemistry and regulatory attention

🌊

Compatibility first

A grade that works for one solvent can fail in another. Match the material to the fluid, not the habit.

🌧️

Batch changeovers

Reactors are opened, cleaned and recharged often. Threads that open and close repeatedly need anti-seize and care.

⚡

Heat cycles on jackets and dryers

Steam and thermic-fluid jackets heat and cool every batch, which loosens joints over time.

⚙️

Audits and effluent scrutiny

Plants are inspected, and effluent systems carry particular attention. Keep records of grade and thread.

⏱️

Hot, dry summers

Temperatures above 40 degrees bake sealants. Use sealants rated for the temperature.

Field Fitting Advice

Fitting Stainless Nipples in Sangareddy–Patancheru Without Headaches

Practical field habits that save plant hours and prevent leaks

🔧

Tip 1

Use a sealant compatible with the fluid. Check against solvents, acids and temperature.

🔧

Tip 2

Use anti-seize on stainless threads you will reopen.

🔧

Tip 3

Don't over-tighten. Tapered threads seal by wedging, and stainless can gall.

🔧

Tip 4

Clean and degrease threads and bores before commissioning.

🔧

Tip 5

Leak-test with an appropriate fluid before introducing process chemicals, following your procedure.

🔧

Tip 6

Record the grade and thread of replaced parts for the next audit.

🔧

Tip 7

Mark thread type on mixed BSPT and NPT lines.
