Regional Analysis

Coal, Boilers, Ash and Scrubbers

Why Singrauli buys nipples by the overhaul list and by the thousand

Singrauli's coal mines are run by a large public-sector coal company, and the district hosts some of the biggest thermal power stations in the country, including a vast central-sector plant at Vindhyachal, an ultra-mega plant at Sasan and several private and state stations. Further along the border, Anpara and the Rihand area add more. Each station runs boilers, turbines, coal-handling plants, ash-handling systems, demineralised-water plants and cooling circuits, and each has an army of contractors for maintenance.

Two things are newer. Flue-gas desulphurisation systems, which use limestone slurry to clean exhaust, are being retrofitted at power plants across India, adding large new piping systems with abrasive slurries. And plants are upgrading controls and instrumentation, adding small-bore instrument lines in volume.

Mines add their own demand. Draglines, shovels, dumpers and crushers run on hydraulics, lubrication and compressed air, in dust and vibration that would ruin ordinary fittings.

Buying Guide

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for mine, power and overhaul-contractor buyers

01

What are the temperature and pressure?

Steam and hot-water lines need a heavier wall, the right grade and a sealant rated for the temperature. Send the figures, and ask your design engineer to confirm what a threaded joint may carry.

02

Is it in slurry?

Ash and limestone slurries are abrasive and wear fittings from inside. Choose a sturdy wall and inspect nipples on a schedule.

03

When does the overhaul start?

Parts must be on site before the window opens. Send the full list and date, and we will say honestly what we can meet.

04

Is it a spare for old equipment?

Many mines and plants run long-serving machines. Send the worn nipple or a measured sketch, and we will machine a match.

Local Realities

What Coal Dust, Fly Ash and 45-Degree Heat Do to a Nipple

Five realities of an energy belt

Heat above 45 degrees

Summer heat expands pipework and bakes sealants. Use sealants rated for the temperature and re-check joints after hot spells.

Coal dust and fly ash

Fine, abrasive dust grinds into threads and wears fittings. Wipe clean before assembly, and keep spares sealed.

Abrasive slurries

Ash and limestone slurries wear small-bore fittings quickly. Inspect and replace on a schedule.

Overhaul calendars

Planned overhauls have fixed dates, and missing small parts delay restart. Send lists weeks ahead.

Very long supply lines

Singrauli is far from the big fitting markets. Holding a few extra fast-moving sizes is cheap insurance.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Nipples on Singrauli Plants

Habits that save downtime, prevent callbacks and ensure leak-free runs

  • Clean threads before every assembly. Dust and old sealant cause leaks.
  • Use a high-temperature sealant on steam lines, and anti-seize on stainless threads.
  • Don't over-tighten. Tapered threads seal by wedging, and heat adds stress.
  • Allow for expansion with supports or flexible sections.
  • Re-check joints after the first heat cycles.
  • Record the grade and thread of replaced parts for the next audit.
  • Keep spares sealed and dry in a clean store.
