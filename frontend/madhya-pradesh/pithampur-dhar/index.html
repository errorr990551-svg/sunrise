Vehicles, Forgings, Test Tracks and a New Textile Park

Why Pithampur buys nipples for heat-treat shops, paint shops and press lines

Pithampur Industrial Area, about 45 km from Indore, is one of India's largest industrial estates. Commercial-vehicle, tractor and passenger-vehicle makers, engine, axle and transmission plants, and a large layer of tier-1 and tier-2 suppliers occupy its sectors. Forging and casting shops supply heavy parts, press shops stamp body panels, and heat-treatment and surface-finishing units serve them all. A high-speed test track nearby supports vehicle development. An SEZ adjoining the estate hosts pharma, apparel and other export units, and the PM MITRA textile park near Dhar is bringing integrated spinning, weaving, processing and garmenting onto one campus.

That makes Pithampur's pipework heavy on utilities. Paint shops run DM water and heated pretreatment tanks. Heat-treatment lines run quench and cooling circuits. Press shops and assembly lines run compressed air and hydraulics. Forges and foundries run cooling water and coolant.

The common demand is for sameness. The part that fit last quarter has to fit this one.

Buying Guide

Four Questions Before You Order

Written for plants that run to takt time

01

Which revision is the drawing?

Auto buyers work with revision-controlled drawings. Send the revision with your request, so we machine to the right one.

02

What tolerance and finish?

Tell us thread class, length tolerance and surface finish. If your inspector gauges the thread, tell us. We plan inspection around what you check.

03

Is it a repeat part?

Tell us annual quantity and call-off rhythm. We can hold stock and dispatch on schedule.

04

Do you need stainless?

Pithampur's inland climate means 304 stainless is plenty for most water and coolant lines, and 316 is for chemistry. Compressed-air lines collect condensate, so stainless or brass often outlast mild steel. For dry painted headers, MS can still be right.

Maintenance Tips

Fitting Nipples on Pithampur–Dhar Plants

Habits that save downtime, prevent callbacks and ensure leak-free runs

  • Fit drain points at low spots in compressed-air lines.
  • Use sealant suited to the fluid. PTFE tape works for air and water, but check compatibility for oils, coolants and quench media.
  • Use a high-temperature sealant near furnaces and quench tanks.
  • Avoid over-tightening into cast or die-cast housings.
  • Use anti-seize on stainless threads that will be opened again.
  • Support long drops. Vibration from presses and compressors loosens joints.
  • Label thread types on mixed BSPT and NPT lines.
